Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled when you contact Express Safari.

Information you share

When you contact us, you may choose to share your name, contact details, trip choice, travel date, hotel area and group details so we can answer your request.

How it is used

We use the information you provide to answer questions, arrange requested trips and communicate booking details. WhatsApp and social platforms process messages under their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information from our direct conversations where applicable. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for your trip.
