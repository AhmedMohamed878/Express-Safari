About this experience

Sharm el Naga — A relaxed beach-and-snorkeling day

Sharm el Naga is built around a day spent on the beach and snorkeling over the coral reefs. The sheet lists it as a daily trip with entry, snorkeling equipment, lunch and soft drinks all included.

Unlike the boat-based island trips, the source describes this one as a beach day, with no boat ride listed. Pickup is at 8:00 am and you are back at your hotel around 8:00 pm.

It suits guests who want to unwind by the sea with snorkeling as the main activity.

Highlights

  • Full day at the beach
  • Snorkeling over coral reefs
  • Snorkeling equipment included
  • Entry included
  • Lunch and soft drinks included
  • Daily departures

Itinerary

  1. Hotel pickup at 8:00 am.
  2. Transfer to Sharm el Naga.
  3. Beach time and snorkeling at the coral reefs.
  4. Lunch and soft drinks.
  5. Return to your hotel around 8:00 pm.

What's included

  • Hotel transfer (to and from)
  • Entry
  • Snorkeling equipment
  • Lunch
  • Soft drinks

Pickup information

Pickup 8:00 am, return about 8:00 pm.

What to bring

Swimwear, towel, sunscreen, hat and sandals.

Frequently asked questions

  • Is entry included? Yes.
  • What time do we return? About 8:00 pm.
