About this experience

Soar high above the sparkling Red Sea on an exhilarating parasailing flight! Towed behind a specially designed speed boat, rise up to 100 meters in the air and take in breathtaking panoramic views of Hurghada's coastline, desert mountains, and coral reefs. Available as single or double tandem flight.

What's included

  • 10–15 minutes of exhilarating parasailing flight time
  • Option for single flyer or double/tandem flight with a partner
  • Full safety briefing and professional flight crew
  • USCG approved life jackets and safety harness
  • Speedboat transfer to and from parasail launch station
  • Round-trip hotel transfers included

Schedule & Timings

Available daily with flexible departure hours throughout the day from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM.

Before you go

No prior experience needed. Wear swimwear or light clothes. Waterproof cameras are welcome.

All programs include transfer from and to hotel! Private trips available on request.
