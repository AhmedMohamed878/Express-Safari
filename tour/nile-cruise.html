Nile Cruise from Luxor — Four days of temples, with an optional Abu Simbel day

4 or 5 days · Early start, 5:00 am pickup · Food and room on board · All entries included except Abu Simbel · Drinks not included

This is a four-day Nile cruise that starts in Luxor and passes the temples at Esna, Edfu and Kom Ombo on the way to Aswan. Food and a single or double room on board are included, and every entry fee is covered except Abu Simbel.

Day one is a full Luxor sightseeing day, from Karnak Temple to the Valley of the Kings, before you check in to the ship. The final day finishes with a museum visit and Elephant Island in Aswan.

You can end the program on day four or stay one more night on the boat for a fifth day at Abu Simbel. Express Safari states it can arrange all trips privately.

Highlights

  • Karnak Temple, Hatshepsut Temple, Valley of the Kings and the Colossi of Memnon on day one
  • Lunch with a view over the Nile, Luxor Temple and Karnak Temple
  • Esna Temple, Edfu Temple and Kom Ombo
  • Museum and Elephant Island in Aswan
  • Optional fifth day to Abu Simbel
  • All entries included except Abu Simbel

Itinerary — 4-day program

  1. Day 1 — Luxor. 5:00 am pickup from your hotel and transfer to Luxor. Visit Karnak Temple. Cross the Nile to the west bank. Lunch with a view over the Nile, Luxor Temple and Karnak Temple. Visit Hatshepsut Temple, the Valley of the Kings and the Colossi of Memnon. Go to the Nile cruise and check in.
  2. Day 2 — Esna. Sail from Luxor to Esna. Visit Esna Temple. Lunch on the boat. Afternoon city tour in Esna.
  3. Day 3 — Edfu. Visit Edfu Temple. Lunch on the ship. City tour in Edfu.
  4. Day 4 — Kom Ombo. City tour. Lunch on the ship, then continue to Aswan. Visit the museum and Elephant Island. Return to Hurghada by train to Luxor, then by bus to Hurghada.

Itinerary — 5-day option

Stay one more night on the boat. Day 5: see Abu Simbel, then return to Hurghada by train to Luxor and bus to Hurghada. Abu Simbel entry is not included.

What's included

  • Food on board
  • Single or double room on board
  • All entry fees except Abu Simbel
  • Hotel pickup in Hurghada (per the sheet header)
  • Temple and sightseeing program as listed

What's not included

  • Entry to Abu Simbel (5th-day option)
  • Drinks

These two are stated on the sheet.

Pickup information

Day 1 pickup is at 5:00 am from your hotel in Hurghada, with transfer to Luxor. The return is by train to Luxor and bus to Hurghada.

What to bring

Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection, a light layer, and money for drinks, which are not included.

Frequently asked questions

  • How long is it? 4 days, or 5 with the Abu Simbel option.
  • Is food included? Yes, food on board and lunches as listed.
  • Are drinks included? No.
  • Is Abu Simbel included? No, entry is not included and it is only on the 5-day option.
  • Can it be private? Express Safari states all trips can be private.
