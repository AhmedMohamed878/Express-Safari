About this experience

The ultimate island-hopping adventure! Visit three distinct island stops across the Red Sea in a single thrilling cruise. Snorkel along vibrant outer barrier reefs, swim in crystal turquoise lagoons, and enjoy open lunch and drinks on board.

What's included

  • Full-day cruise stopping at three island locations
  • Multiple guided snorkeling stops at top coral reefs
  • Complete snorkeling gear (masks, snorkels, fins)
  • Water sports fun (banana boat & sofa ride where available)
  • Fresh open buffet lunch & soft drinks on board
  • Round-trip hotel transfer included

Schedule & Timings

Daily from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM with hotel collection and drop-off.
