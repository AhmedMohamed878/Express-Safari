About this experience

Luxor 1 Day by Bus — Kings or Queens, with a guide in your language

Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday · 4:30 am – 11:30 pm · By bus · Entries, guide and lunch included

This is a long one-day trip to Luxor from Hurghada by bus, running on Sundays, Tuesdays and Thursdays. The day leaves at 4:30 am and returns at 11:30 pm, so it is a full day with a very early start and late finish.

You choose between a Kings tour and a Queens tour. The Valley of the Kings or the Valley of the Queens is visited depending on your chosen trip.

Entry fees, a guide in your language and lunch are included.

Highlights

  • Karnak Temple
  • Colossi of Memnon
  • Temple of Hatshepsut
  • Valley of the Kings or Valley of the Queens, depending on your trip
  • Guide in your language
  • Entries and lunch included

Itinerary

  1. Early pickup (4:30 am program start; time confirmed at booking).
  2. Bus transfer to Luxor.
  3. Karnak Temple.
  4. Colossi of Memnon.
  5. Temple of Hatshepsut.
  6. Valley of the Kings or Valley of the Queens, depending on your chosen trip.
  7. Lunch.
  8. Bus return to Hurghada (program ends around 11:30 pm).

What's included

  • Bus transfer
  • Entry fees
  • Guide in your language
  • Lunch

Pickup information

Program 4:30 am – 11:30 pm on Sundays, Tuesdays and Thursdays.

What to bring

Comfortable walking shoes, a hat, sunscreen, water, and a light layer for the early morning.

Frequently asked questions

  • Which days does it run? Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday.
  • Which valley do I visit? The Valley of the Kings or the Valley of the Queens, depending on the trip you choose.
  • How long is the day? From 4:30 am to 11:30 pm.
