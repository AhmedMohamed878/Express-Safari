About this experience

Orange Bay — Two snorkeling stops, island entry and lunch on board

Orange Bay is a full-day boat trip from Hurghada built around the water. You head out on our boat, stop twice to snorkel at coral reefs, and spend the rest of the day cruising and relaxing between swims.

Everything you need for a straightforward day is covered: snorkeling equipment, lunch and soft drinks on board, and entry to the island. Your hotel transfer is included, so the day starts and ends at your door.

If you want a classic Red Sea snorkeling day without extra planning, this is a simple, well-rounded choice.

Highlights

  • Two snorkeling stops at coral reefs, giving you two chances to get in the water rather than one
  • Snorkeling equipment included
  • Island entry included
  • Lunch and soft drinks on board
  • Hotel transfer included
  • Can be arranged as a private trip

Itinerary

  1. Hotel pickup (time confirmed at booking).
  2. Transfer to the boat and departure.
  3. Enjoy the boat ride.
  4. First Snorkeling Stop — explore the coral reef scenery at the first stop.
  5. Second Snorkeling Stop — get back in the water for another snorkeling stop at the coral reefs.
  6. Lunch and soft drinks on board.
  7. Island visit, with entry included.
  8. Return to your hotel (the program runs until about 5:00 pm).

What's included

  • Hotel transfer (to and from)
  • Boat trip
  • Two snorkeling stops at coral reefs
  • Snorkeling equipment
  • Lunch on board
  • Soft drinks on board
  • Island entry

Pickup information

Hotel transfer is included. Operating hours are listed as 8:00 am – 5:00 pm.

What to bring

Swimwear, a towel, sunscreen and a hat are sensible for a day on the water.

Frequently asked questions

  • How many times do we snorkel? Two snorkeling stops at coral reefs.
  • Is equipment provided? Yes, snorkeling equipment is included.
  • Is lunch included? Yes, lunch and soft drinks are served on board.
  • Is island entry included? Yes.
  • Can we book privately? Express Safari states all trips can be made private.
